Undergraduate Thesis
Performance Evaluation of Concrete Using Zeolite as Partial
Replacement for Cement under CO₂ Curing
- Investigated concrete mixtures containing 0--20% natural zeolite
  under accelerated CO₂ curing.
- Evaluated compressive and tensile strength, ultrasonic pulse
  velocity (UPV), water absorption, rapid chloride permeability
  (RCPT), and carbonation depth.
- Identified 10% zeolite replacement as the optimum in the reported
  study.
