Undergraduate Thesis (B.Sc.)
Nov 2024 – Jun 2025
"Performance Evaluation of Concrete Using Zeolite as Partial Replacement for Cement under CO2 Curing"
  • Studied concrete with 0–20% natural zeolite replacement under accelerated CO2 curing.
  • Evaluated compressive & tensile strength, UPV, water absorption, RCPT, and carbonation depth.
  • Identified 10% zeolite replacement as optimal (+9% strength, 45% reduction in chloride permeability).
