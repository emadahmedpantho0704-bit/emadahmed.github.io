Profile Summary

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh, where I graduated second in my class with Honours (Dean's Award recipient) and am currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Structural Engineering. My academic background centers on structural engineering, sustainable concrete, and computational modeling, combining undergraduate teaching and structural design (ETABS, AutoCAD) with experimental and data-driven research.

For my B.Sc. thesis, I evaluated concrete with 0–20% natural zeolite replacement under conventional water curing versus 24-hour early carbonation followed by water curing at 28 and 56 days, identifying 10% replacement as optimal for strength gain, reduced permeability, and UPV correlation, supported by TGA and FTIR characterization. In my M.Sc. research, I am developing a two-stage machine learning framework for seismic vulnerability screening and retrofit decision support on 350 RVA reinforced concrete buildings in Dhaka using BNBC-2020 ETABS response spectrum targets.

Master's Thesis

Machine Learning-Based Seismic Vulnerability Assessment of Existing Buildings in Dhaka: Bridging Visual Screening with the Damaged State of the Building
Stage: M.Sc. Status: Ongoing (2025–Present) | Candidate: Emad Ahmed | Supervisor: Dr. Md. Basir Zisan
Proposes a two-stage machine learning framework on 350 Rapid Visual Assessment (RVA) reinforced concrete buildings in Dhaka using ETABS Linear Dynamic (Response Spectrum) Analysis targets under BNBC-2020 (MIDR and DCR-failed walls and columns).
  • Model 1 predicts baseline MIDR and DCR-failed members to flag Safe (< 2.5% MIDR and zero member failures) vs. Not Safe buildings.
  • Model 2 recommends retrofit measures and estimates planning-stage cost (Tk.) for Not Safe buildings, audited with stratified cross-validation and SHAP.
Key areas: Seismic vulnerability screening, BNBC-2020, ETABS, retrofit decision support, SHAP
Explanatory walkthrough of the ongoing M.Sc. thesis proposal on seismic vulnerability screening and retrofit decision support for RC buildings in Dhaka.

Undergraduate Thesis (B.Sc.)

Performance Evaluation of Concrete Using Zeolite as a Partial Replacement for Cement under CO2 Curing
Stage: B.Sc. Status: Completed (Nov 2024 – Jun 2025) | Supervisor: Dr. Md. Moinul Islam
Investigated concrete with 0–20% natural zeolite replacement under accelerated CO2 curing, evaluating mechanical properties, durability, and microstructural characterization.
  • Tested compressive & tensile strength, UPV, water absorption, RCPT, carbonation depth, TGA, and FTIR.
  • Identified 10% zeolite under hybrid CO2 curing as optimal, boosting compressive strength by 15.0% (up to 38.42 MPa) and cutting chloride permeability by 20.5%.
Key areas: Natural zeolite, CO2 curing, UPV, RCPT, TGA/FTIR, sustainable concrete
