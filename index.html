Profile Summary

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) (Ranked 2nd in the department), with a background in structural engineering and sustainable concrete. Experienced in teaching, research, and structural design using ETABS and AutoCAD, my work bridges fundamental structural mechanics with practical engineering education.

My current research focuses on CO2 reduction in concrete, machine learning applications in civil engineering, and data-driven structural and seismic vulnerability assessment of existing buildings.
